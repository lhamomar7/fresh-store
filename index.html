About

Pizza HaMesholash was founded in 1992 as a small local pizza place with a big passion for quality and taste. What started as a modest neighborhood shop quickly became a favorite spot for many customers thanks to its fresh ingredients and dedication to great service.

Over the years, we expanded and opened new branches across the country, bringing our unique flavor to more people and becoming a well-known name loved by pizza fans everywhere.

As we grew, we also expanded our menu. From classic pizzas to a wider variety of toppings and dishes, we made sure to offer something for everyone while keeping the same quality and love that defined us from the beginning.
